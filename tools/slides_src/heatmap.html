14 · Attention heatmaps

Each panel is one description. Rows are the regex tokens the model generated, columns are the input words, and brighter means more attention. The count attends to the number in the sentence; each literal slot attends to its quoted word; the tilde attends to not, the ampersand to and; the vowel class attends to vowel. The bottom right panel is the most telling: the sentence mentions blue first, but the regex must start with red, and the decoder indeed looks at the last word first. That reordering is impossible to do well with a single summary vector.
