6 · Preprocessing

Literal text is the one thing the model cannot know in advance, since a user can ask for any word. So before the model sees a description, every quoted literal is replaced by a numbered slot, in order of appearance. The model learns to put the slots in the right place in the regex, and afterwards we put the real words back, escaping special characters. The rest is standard: lowercase word tokens with a vocabulary built from the training split only, and character-level regex tokens with the four special tokens required by the course.
