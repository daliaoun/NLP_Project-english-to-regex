From English to regular expressions

An encoder-decoder with attention, built from scratch and trained on a verified dataset

English

lines containing ‘cat’ at least 2 times

Regular expression

(.*cat){2,}.*

Mohamed Ali Aoun

Welcome. This project builds a sequence-to-sequence model from scratch that reads an English description of a set of text lines and writes the regular expression for it. The example on the slide is the whole task in one picture: English on the left, the regex on the right. I will explain why we built our own verified dataset, then the model, the experiments, and finish with a live demo in the browser.
