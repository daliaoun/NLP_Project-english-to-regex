15 · Examples

Left: three exact matches, one in the training style, one with a held-out sentence pattern and one handwritten, where the model also has to reorder the two literals. Right: three failures, and they are instructive. Have Alice at the end is a held-out construction, and the model reads it as starts with. No fewer than three times contains the words fewer than, and the model follows them, flipping the meaning. No digits at all uses a negation the generator never produces. In every case the output is a valid regex that means something else.
