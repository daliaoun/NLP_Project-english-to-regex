9 · Training

All models are small, so everything trains on a CPU. The loss is token-level cross-entropy with padding ignored. With teacher forcing at 1.0 the decoder is always fed the gold previous token during training. The best epoch is chosen by validation exact match with early stopping. Besides normal dropout we replace five percent of the input words by the unknown token during training, so the model learns not to rely on every single word. The attention model trained in 25 minutes and stopped after 11 epochs; its best epoch was the seventh.
