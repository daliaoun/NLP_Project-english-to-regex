4 · VeriRegex

The generator first draws a meaning, a small tree of conditions such as at least two times cat. From that one tree it writes the English, choosing among many phrasings, and the regular expression. Separately, the same tree is evaluated by a plain Python function that never looks at the regex. On roughly three hundred probe strings the regex, run through our own matcher, and the Python function must give the same answer every time; a single disagreement stops the build. We also throw away meanings that accept every line or no line, such as starts with a digit and starts with a letter, 5,251 of them.
