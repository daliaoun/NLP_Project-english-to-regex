13 · Training curves

Left: dashed lines are training loss, solid lines validation loss. Middle: validation exact match per epoch. Right: validation BLEU. The attention model is at 89 percent exact match after one epoch and above 99 percent after four. The baseline climbs over about ten epochs, reaches 94 percent at epoch 14 and 96 percent at epoch 28. The 15-epoch baseline is the first half of the same run, so its curve is hidden under the longer one. Training and validation losses stay close for both models, so there is no sign of overfitting on this data; validation loss is even lower than training loss because dropout is only active during training.
