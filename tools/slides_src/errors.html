16 · Error analysis

On the held-out phrasings, 1,239 of 1,500 outputs are exact and 26 more are equivalent. All 235 wrong outputs are valid regexes, so the syntax is learned; the errors are about meaning. Two thirds have the wrong structure, mostly one condition read as another, typically a held-out construction such as have X at the end read as a construction the model knows. Most of the rest are negations: no fewer than is read as fewer than. On new regexes in the training style only 11 outputs are wrong, and six of them are the same unseen combination, ends with X or Y. The model has learned constructions, not the meaning of each word on its own.
