The task

Describe the lines in English, get the regex

lines where ‘red’ comes before ‘blue’

.*red.*blue.*

lines that start with a capital letter and end with a number

([A-Z].*)&(.*[0-9])

lines that contain ‘error’ and do not contain ‘warning’

(.*error.*)&(~(.*warning.*))

A sequence-to-sequence problem: one token sequence in, another out. The regex has to match whole lines, & means and, ~ means not, and the model has no pretrained weights.

2 · The task

Three outputs of the final model, all exactly right. The input is a plain English description of the lines to match, and the output is a regular expression that must match the whole line. The dialect has two operators that ordinary regular expressions lack: ampersand for and, tilde for not. They make combinations of conditions easy to write, and our own matcher can run them.
