12 · What we learned

Four lessons. First, attention removes six out of seven of the baseline's errors on new regexes, even when the baseline is trained twice as long. Second, the baseline's errors are concentrated in combinations of conditions, which is exactly the bottleneck attention addresses: a single vector must carry two or three conditions with their literals and numbers. Third, attention also trains much faster. Fourth, and most important for honesty: on sentence patterns the model never saw, it loses about seventeen points. And on that harder test the long baseline is only 1.4 points behind, so what limits both models there is what they learned from the templates. Beam search, by the way, does not change the attention model's outputs.
