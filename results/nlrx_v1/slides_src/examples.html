Left: three of the exact matches on test descriptions the model never saw. Right: three failures, and they are instructive. In the first, the model drops the negation and swaps intersection for alternation. In the second, it gets all the pieces but groups them wrongly, so the scope of not changes. In the third, it applies the repetition count to the wrong part of the expression. In every case the output is a valid regex that means something different, which is why failures are semantic, not syntactic.
