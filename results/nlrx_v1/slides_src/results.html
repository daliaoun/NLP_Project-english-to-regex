9 · Results

This is the main table. The baseline is a plain LSTM encoder-decoder; we trained it for 30 epochs on two seeds and again for up to 60 epochs, because it was still improving at 30. Even the longer-trained baseline stays well below the attention model: 34 versus 41 percent exact match and 56 versus 62 percent functional accuracy. The last row, the best model, adds the second set of templated descriptions for the training regexes and more dropout. Results for the paper this dataset comes from, 58 percent under a stricter equivalence check, are in the same ballpark as our functional accuracy but not directly comparable.
