The task

Describe the lines in English, get the regex

lines with ‘dog’ before a capital letter followed by a lower-case letter

(dog).*([A-Z].*[a-z].*).*

lines not words with lower-case letter, 7 or more times

~(\b([a-z]){7,}\b)

Items with a numeral or an upper case letter at least five times.

(([A-Z])|([0-9])){5,}

A sequence-to-sequence problem: one token sequence in, another out. The regex has to match whole lines, and the model has no pretrained weights.

Three real examples from the test set, all of which the model gets exactly right. Note that the descriptions are written by crowd workers, so the wording is loose and sometimes odd. The regex dialect also has two operators that ordinary regular expressions lack: ampersand for intersection and tilde for complement, which matters later when we check regexes by behaviour.
