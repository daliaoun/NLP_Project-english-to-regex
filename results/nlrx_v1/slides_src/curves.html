The attention runs jump up in the first few epochs, while the two baseline runs sit near zero exact match for about eight epochs and then climb slowly. The baseline gap is larger, 0.11 after 30 epochs and 0.15 after 60, so it overfits more, not less, even though it is the smaller model.
