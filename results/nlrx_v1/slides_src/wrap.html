17 · Conclusion

To close: a from-scratch LSTM encoder-decoder does learn the task, with valid regexes 99.9 percent of the time. Attention is the single most useful addition, adding 7 to 9 points of exact match, and extra paraphrase data with more dropout gives the best model at 45.3 percent exact match and 65.1 percent functional accuracy. The remaining errors are about negation and grouping, so the natural next step is structure-aware decoding. One lesson: the metric matters as much as the model, since exact match alone understates quality when many outputs are equivalent. Thank you, happy to take questions.
