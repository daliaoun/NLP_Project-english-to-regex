12 · Attention heatmaps

Each panel is one test sentence. Rows are the regex tokens the model generated, columns are the input words, and brighter means more attention. While writing the long vowel class, the decoder attends almost entirely to the word vowel. The repetition counter attends to the number and to the words more and times. The complement operator attends to the word not. This is the kind of evidence that the model is learning an alignment between English phrases and regex pieces, not just memorising patterns.
