10 · What helped

Reading the table row by row. Attention is the clear win. The extra data and dropout help further, but that is a single run, so treat it as indicative. The bidirectional encoder and the teacher-forcing ratio of 0.5 did not give a clear gain, and 0.5 also produced more syntactically invalid regexes. Beam search with width 5 changed results by about a point in either direction; it guarantees valid output but does not find better regexes, which suggests the remaining errors come from the model’s own ranking rather than the search.
