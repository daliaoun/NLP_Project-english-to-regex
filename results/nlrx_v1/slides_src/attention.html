The improvement: attention

The decoder looks back at every input word

1

Score

How relevant is each input word to the next output token?

score = v^T tanh(W_s s + W_h h_i)

2

Weights

Softmax over the input positions. Padding gets zero weight.

3

Context

Weighted sum of the encoder states, fed to the LSTM and to the output layer.

Why one vector is not enough

Without attention, 256 numbers must carry the whole description, and every regex token depends on that single summary.

6 · Attention

Our one planned improvement is additive attention. At each decoding step, the decoder state is compared with every encoder state to get a score, the scores are turned into weights with a softmax, and the weighted sum becomes a context vector that is used both as an extra LSTM input and in the output layer. Padding positions are masked out. The motivation is the bottleneck of a single fixed-size vector. The number on the right is concrete evidence from the logs: after two epochs the attention model already gets 20 percent of validation regexes exactly right, while the baseline is still at zero.
