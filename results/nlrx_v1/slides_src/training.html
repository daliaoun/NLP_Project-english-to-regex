7 · Training

All models are small, so everything trains on a CPU. The loss is token-level cross-entropy with padding ignored. With teacher forcing at 1.0 the decoder is always fed the gold previous token during training; one experiment lowers it to 0.5, where the model sometimes feeds itself its own prediction. The best epoch is chosen by validation exact match with early stopping. The best model adds the templated descriptions of the training regexes and uses more dropout.
